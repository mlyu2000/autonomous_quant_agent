SMB Capital Trend Following with EMAs

Our flagship trend following system employs two exponential moving averages: the 20‑period EMA and the 50‑period EMA on the 4‑hour chart. The entry signal occurs when the 20 EMA crosses above the 50 EMA and the price is above both averages, confirming an uptrend. The setup requires a clear directional bias; we look for a series of higher highs and higher lows on the higher timeframe. The initial stop loss is set at 1.5 times the Average True Range (ATR) below the entry. Profit is captured using a trailing stop of 3× ATR. We maintain a constant position size, risking exactly 2% of our trading capital per trade. This strategy shines in strong trending markets; in consolidation periods we typically see whipsaws and small losses, so we monitor the Average Directional Index (ADX) – values above 25 indicate a viable trend.
