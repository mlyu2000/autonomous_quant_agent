SMB Capital Mean Reversion Strategy using Bollinger Bands

This mean reversion strategy operates on the daily chart. We use Bollinger Bands with 20‑day SMA and 2 standard deviations. The setup condition is price touching the lower band, indicating oversold conditions. The entry trigger is a bullish candlestick pattern – specifically a hammer or a bullish engulfing – occurring at the lower band. The initial stop loss is placed 2% below the entry price. The take profit target is the middle band (the 20‑day SMA). Position sizing is reduced by half when the overall market is in a strong downtrend (as measured by the 200‑day EMA). The strategy is most effective in range‑bound markets with low to moderate volatility. We avoid this setup when the Bollinger Bands are expanding rapidly, which often signals a strong trend and increases the risk of continuation against our mean‑reversion position.
