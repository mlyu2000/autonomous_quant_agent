SMB Capital Iron Condor Options Strategy

This is a defined‑risk, non‑directional options strategy used around high‑volatility events like earnings. The setup is when implied volatility (IV) is elevated above its 30‑day average, and we anticipate the underlying price to remain within a range. We sell an out‑of‑the‑money call spread and an out‑of‑the‑money put spread, both with approximately 16 delta, expiring in about 30 days. The net credit received must be at least $1.50 per contract (or $150 per spread). The maximum loss is limited to the width of the spreads minus the credit. Position sizing: we allocate no more than 5% of our capital to any single iron condor. Risk management: if the underlying price moves within 2 points of either short strike, we consider adjusting the position (e.g., rolling the threatened side). The primary exit is when the position reaches 50% of its maximum profit, or we close before expiration to avoid gamma risk.
