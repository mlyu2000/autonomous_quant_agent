SMB Capital Breakout Strategy with Volume Confirmation

We identify consolidation periods on the 15‑minute chart. A valid setup is a range of at least 10 consecutive bars where price is bounded between a clear support and resistance. The breakout trigger is a decisive move above the resistance level (or below support for shorts) accompanied by volume that is at least 1.5 times the 20‑period average volume. On the breakout, we enter immediately. The stop loss is placed just below the consolidation low (or above the high for shorts). The take profit target is a fixed 3:1 reward‑to‑risk ratio; for example, if the distance from entry to stop is 0.5%, we target a 1.5% move in our direction. Additionally, we scale out half of the position when we achieve 2× the initial risk (2R) and move the stop to breakeven. The strategy performs best in emerging trends or high‑momentum regimes; we avoid breakouts during low‑volume, choppy markets.
